The projections

Independent Poisson goals over 90 minutes. UCL scoring/conceding averages are shrunk toward 1.5 goals per team with a ten-match prior and a 0.1-goal home adjustment. The current-season sample is small. Expected goals shown here are model scoring means, not shot-based xG.

The prices

Fair decimal odds = 1 ÷ model probability, without bookmaker margin. They are mathematical reference prices, not Bet365 quotes. Without current sportsbook prices, EV and betting value cannot be confirmed.

The travel estimates

Previous match venue and current venue come from ESPN. Distances use public city-center coordinates as proxies, not exact stadium routing. Flight-time proxy = straight-line distance ÷ 700 km/h; the second estimate adds a hypothetical two-hour airport buffer. Actual flights, road routes, transfers and whether teams returned home are unknown. Same-city travel time is not inferred.

The lineups

A starting XI appears only if ESPN lists eleven unique starters for the correct event and team. Pending means unavailable, not that players are injured. These lists are source-reported; no unverified predicted XI is invented. Lineup information does not change the frozen forecast.
